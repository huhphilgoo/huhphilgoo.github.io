Five tips that really draw differently

Round, pencil, flat, highlighter and calligraphy. The highlighter deepens where strokes overlap; the calligraphy nib thickens and thins with the direction you pull it.

  • Each tip remembers its own size
  • Tip and size in one panel
  • The eraser keeps its own size too

Wobbly in, clean out

Draw a line, circle, ellipse, rectangle, triangle or star and pause before lifting the pen — it straightens out, at whatever angle you drew it.

  • One undo brings your drawing back
  • Lines you meant to be wavy stay wavy
  • Finger or pen

Fill a closed area in one tap

Tap inside an outline and only that area fills. The fill stays fixed in the drawing, so you can draw over it or erase it.

  • Fills from the colour you tapped
  • Clean right up to the line
  • Undo works as usual

Words on the drawing, too

Six Korean typefaces plus the system one. Drag to move, pinch to resize, twist to rotate. Text is part of the drawing, so the eraser takes it off.

  • Its own text colour
  • Two fingers to size and rotate
  • Erasable like any stroke

Unfold it, and the board grows

On an unfolded foldable the toolbar stays in view and the board takes the width of the screen. Fold and unfold — your drawing stays exactly where it was.

  • iPhone Duo
  • Wide inner screens like Galaxy Z Fold
  • Tablets keep the original layout

Privacy

Your drawings stay on your device.

There is no server to upload them to, and no account to attach them to. Everything is handled on the device and is gone when you delete the app. The app is free and carries ads, which means Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics collect the usual advertising identifier and device information — all of it written out in the privacy policy.
