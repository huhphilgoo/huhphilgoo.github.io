This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application bshort (listed as “bshort - Aesthetic Shorts”, package com.huhfeelgood.bshort), developed and published by Heo Philgoo (허필구). Last updated: August 18, 2026.

1. What this app touches

bshort works with the photos and videos you select and any music you import yourself. These files are used only to compose your video and never leave your device. The developer cannot see them, and there is no server for them to be sent to.

The app holds no permission to read your photo library. Selection is handled by the system photo picker, which runs in its own process and hands the app only the items you chose. The app never learns that your other photos exist.

Everything involved in making the video — colour grading, typography, music, encoding — happens on the device. None of it uses the network, so it works exactly the same in airplane mode.

2. What the app keeps on your device

The app has no account and creates nothing that identifies you. Only two things are kept inside its own folder:

  • Music you imported — kept in Documents/MusicLibrary so it is there next time. You can delete any track by swiping it in the music list.
  • The most recent video you made — held in Documents/LastRender so the result survives if the app closes before you save it. It is deleted once you save to your photo library or close the result screen.

Both live only on the device and are removed when you delete the app.

4. Advertising

The app is free and carries Google AdMob ads, shown as cards between the templates.

AdMob collects your advertising identifier (IDFA), device information (model, iOS version, language, country), an approximate location derived from your IP address, and a record of ad impressions and clicks. It uses these to serve and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, and — where you have agreed — to personalize them. The processor is Google LLC; their advertising policy has the detail.

Your choice about personalized ads. On first launch, iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Decline it and no identifier is shared for personalization — every feature of the app still works. You can change the choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Apart from fetching ads, the app does not use the network.

5. System APIs the app uses

Of the system APIs Apple requires a declared reason for, this app uses exactly one: reading file timestamps. It reads the creation date of files in its own folder so your imported music can be listed newest first. It does not read files belonging to other apps or to you.

6. Sharing

The developer does not sell or rent your information. Nothing is shared with anyone beyond the processing Google LLC performs under its own policy, as described in section 4.

For deletion requests concerning data collected by a third party, write to pgmagician@gmail.com and you will be walked through it.

7. Children’s privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 14, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
