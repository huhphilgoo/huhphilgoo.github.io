Looks

Same photos, a different film every time.

Four moods — film, golden, blue and vivid. Every template has its own colour grade, type and track, so no two share a look.

More fun

Your photo becomes the scene.

New

Characters stand where you look

The app notices which way the person in your photo is facing and moves the character to that side, so it looks like you shot it together. Face direction is read on the phone and nowhere else.

Seasonal

New templates every season

For seasons like Halloween, limited-time templates arrive without an app update — with moving characters and new transitions.

One shot with a character

Dracula, skeletons and pumpkins stand next to the people in your photos, waving and dancing. You in the middle, the character at your side.

Your photos, words and music

Make your own to set the order, timing and where your words go, then lay a track from a file or from a video underneath.

More on a bigger screen

Unfold a foldable and more templates fit on each row, and videos play whole instead of cropped.

Privacy

Your photos never leave the phone.

There is no server to send them to. Selection, compositing and export all happen on the device, and the app has no account to sign in to. It is free and carries ads, which means Google AdMob collects the usual advertising identifier and device information — all of it written out in the privacy policy.
