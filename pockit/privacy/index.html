This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application K-Pockit (listed as “K-Pockit”, package com.hpg.expense_tracker), developed and published by Heo Philgoo (허필구). Last updated: September 5, 2026.

1. Information collected directly by the developer

The developer does not collect or store any personal information on any server. The app requires no sign-up, login, or account.

Transactions, categories, budgets and payment-method information are stored only in a database on your device and are never transmitted to the developer's server. The app has no sign-up and no account, and the data is deleted with the app. When — and only when — you ask for a currency conversion, a currency code is sent to a public exchange-rate service (api.frankfurter.app); no transaction data or personal information is included. CSV and JSON export runs only when you start it, and the file is written where you choose.

2. Information collected by third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising): advertising identifier (AAID/IDFA), device information (model, OS version, language, country), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data — used to serve and measure ads, prevent ad fraud, and — where you have consented — to personalize ads. Processed by Google LLC. https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Consent. Users in the EEA and the UK are shown a Google UMP consent form on first launch. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency (ATT) lets you allow or deny tracking.

4. Sharing of information

The developer does not sell or rent your personal information. No information is shared other than the processing performed by the third-party services listed in section 2 (Google LLC) under their own policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). For deletion requests concerning third-party data, contact pgmagician@gmail.com.

6. Children’s privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 18, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
