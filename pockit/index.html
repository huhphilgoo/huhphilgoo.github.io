Privacy

Your records stay on your phone.

There is no account to tie records to and no server to send them to. Every transaction lives in a database on the device and disappears with the app. Receipt photos are read on your phone and never saved. What leaves the app: a currency code when it asks for exchange rates, diagnostic information such as device model and app version sent by the receipt reader (Google ML Kit), and — because the app is free and shows a banner ad — the advertising ID and device information collected by Google AdMob. All of it is in the privacy policy.
